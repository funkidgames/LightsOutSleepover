THE SHOT

You are Agent Ace, the best sniper on the Rescue Squad. The sneaky Grabber Gang took hostages all over town. Look through your scope, take out the bad guys with BONK rounds, and save everyone. Just don't hit a hostage!
🪙 0 coins
DIFFICULTY

Goal: knock out every bad guy (masks and beanies) without hitting a hostage (hands up, tied with rope).
Computer: move the mouse to aim, click to fire, right-click / Z / mouse wheel to zoom, hold Shift or Space to hold your breath (the scope stops shaking).
Phone / tablet: drag anywhere to move the scope, tap FIRE, ZOOM and hold HOLD BREATH.
Alert! After your first shot the gang knows you're there. Catch them all before the alert timer runs out!
Decoys: the gang sets up cardboard cutouts of themselves to trick you. They look just like the robbers! Zoom in: decoys stand on a thin wooden peg, never blink, never move and never talk. Hitting one wastes a shot and costs 2 seconds of alert time!
Shield robbers: some robbers carry a shield. Hit the shield until it breaks (the number on it shows how many hits are left), then hit them once more.
Wind: in later levels the wind pushes your shot. Aim into the wind using the little marks in the scope.
Coins: win a level to earn coins (Hard gives the most!). Spend them in the Shop on new snipers and skins.
Robbers you hit get knocked out and taken away by the police. 🚓 Don't want blood? Turn Blood splats off on this screen.
