GOBBLE KING

Gobble food, trap the other snakes and become the biggest snake in the arena. How long can you stay King? 👑

Steer: move your mouse, or drag your finger where you want to go.
Boost: hold the mouse button or Space, or hold 🔥 on a phone. Boosting makes you a little shorter.
Gobble: if a snake's head bumps into your body, it pops into yummy food!
Power-up: every time you gobble a snake you get a power for 20 seconds: ⚡ Turbo, 🛡️ Shield, 🧲 Magnet or 🌟 Double Food.
King: the biggest snake wears the 👑. Stay King as long as you can!
