SLITHER STORM

25 snakes. 1 island. Jump from the Sky Balloon, crack open chests, grab gear and stay out of the purple Hiss Storm. Be the last snake slithering to become TOP SNAKE! 🐍🏆

Move: WASD or arrows · phone: drag on the left side
Shoot: aim with the mouse and click · phone: hold the right side (it aims for you)
Jump from the balloon: Space · phone: JUMP button
Swap gear: 1 2 3 or tap a slot · Pick up / swap: E
Heal: Q eats an 🍎 apple · F drinks a 🫐 shield berry
Map: M or tap the mini map
Chests: 🧺 baskets, ⚪ silver chests, 🟡 golden coil chests, 🟩 snack crates and 🎁 sky drops that float down later. Better chest, better loot!
