How to play

  1. Aliens fly up to the counter. The bubble shows the cupcake they want.
  2. Build it on your plate: tap a cake, then a frosting, then any toppings. Tap a topping again to take it off.
  3. Tap the alien to serve it! Right cupcake = stardust ⭐. Faster = bigger tip.
  4. The green bar is how long they can wait. Serve in a row for a combo!
  5. Reach the stardust goal before closing time to unlock the next day. Spend stardust in the Space Shop.
  6. Golden crown customers are VIPs (Alessandro, Chiara and Chris). They pay double!
