🪶 Feathers: win to earn feathers. Spend them on a Topic clue (like "an animal") or a Letter hint that shows one letter in its spot.

📅 Daily is one new word each day for each level. 🔁 Practice gives you as many words as you want.

Easy = 4 letters and 7 tries (the topic is always shown). Normal = 5 letters, 6 tries. Hard = 6 letters, 6 tries.
