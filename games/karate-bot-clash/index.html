KARATE BOT CLASH

Beat the karate bots! Hit them to charge your ⚡ special move. Every win gives you a 🪙 Fight Token to spend in the shop.

Win a fight: knock out the bot 2 times (best of 3 rounds).
⚡ Special: every hit fills your power bar. When it says READY, press Special!
🛡 Block: hold block to stop hits (specials still sneak a little through).
🪙 Tokens: 1 token for every fight you win. Buy moves, specials, skills and skins in the shop.
🥋 Belt: beat a new bot to unlock the next one and earn a new belt color.

Keyboard: ←→ move, ↑ jump, ↓ block, J punch, K kick, U move A, I move B, L or Space special, P pause. WASD works too.
