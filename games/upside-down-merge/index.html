Upside Down Merge

Drop the characters into the jar. When two of the same touch, they merge into the next character!

Keep merging all the way up to Eleven 🧇, the last character!

Tap or click to drop. On a computer you can also use ← → and Space.
Don't let the pile go over the red line!
